PC Agent

A personal assistant that runs on one home PC.

PC Agent is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own Windows PC and sends its owner short Telegram messages: alerts about important new emails, reminders before calendar events, and daily check-ins.

It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it. It has read-only access to its owner's Gmail and Google Calendar, and only after the owner signs in.
