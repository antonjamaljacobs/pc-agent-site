PC Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

PC Agent is a personal tool used by one person, its developer, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, PC Agent requests read-only access to:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): to notice new emails that need attention.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): to send reminders before events.

It cannot send, delete, or change any email or calendar event.

How the data is used

  • Basic details of new emails (sender, subject, and a short preview) and calendar events are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which decides whether an email matters and writes a short summary.
  • Those short summaries and reminders are sent to the owner's own Telegram account.
  • A small record of which emails and reminders were already handled is stored on the owner's PC. Email contents are not stored.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the services listed above, and only to provide the features described here.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PC Agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
